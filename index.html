ABOUT THE DEVOTIONALS

Scripture for mature everyday faith

These devotionals are written primarily for adults. They address work, relationships, responsibility, character, prayer, decision-making, waiting, grief, hope, integrity and spiritual growth without reducing spiritual growth to one stage of life or one kind of household.

Each day includes a Bible reading reference, a detailed reflection, a closing prayer, a memory verse reference, a hymn suggestion and a concise focus point. Bible quotations and hymn lyrics are not reproduced.
